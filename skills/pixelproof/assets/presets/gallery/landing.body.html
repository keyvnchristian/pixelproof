Questions shop owners ask

When do I get paid?

Every Friday, for all orders completed by the previous Tuesday. The first payout arrives the Friday after your first sale.

Can I bring my products from another platform?

Yes. Upload the export file from your current store and Pixelproof maps the fields for you.

What happens if a customer asks for a refund?

Approve it from the order page. The amount comes out of your next payout, and the customer gets an email right away.
